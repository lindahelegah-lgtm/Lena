About Building Ideas

A young, growing and very vibrant construction company — blending innovation with a culture of creativity to deliver your dream projects.

Our story

From idea to landmark

Building Ideas Limited was founded in 2003, with official registration completed in 2007 at the Registrar-General’s Department in Accra, Ghana. As our name implies, we blend innovation with our culture of creativity.

We provide a wide range of services across the architectural and construction industry, employing graphics that enable our clients to easily comprehend and appreciate every step of our innovative delivery process. We specialise in Project Management, Design & Build, and General & Maintenance Construction — serving individual and corporate entities across Ghana’s sub-region and abroad.

Our Mission

To provide our clients with engineering services for all types of projects — from conceptual planning through to completion — with a highly skilled, professional and experienced team.

Our Aim

To demystify the construction industry in Ghana — giving clients clear, understandable pictures of their projects long before commencement and throughout delivery.

Keys to Success

Delivering professional quality services on time and on budget, and investing heavily in state-of-the-art computing technology across every aspect of our business.
