Our Services

Reliable, holistic and unlimited high-quality remedies for projects of all sizes — for individuals and corporate entities, both in Ghana and abroad.

Core capabilities

Three disciplines, one accountable partner

Project Management

We manage engineering services for all types of projects — from conceptual planning through to completion — with a highly skilled, experienced team. We continuously explore avenues to increase efficiency in delivery while keeping you informed at every step.

  • Feasibility, planning & scheduling
  • Cost estimating & budget control
  • Procurement & contract administration
  • Quality assurance & on-site supervision

Design & Build (Turnkey Projects)

Innovative and economical design services that maintain state-of-the-art design technology. We blend innovation with culture to give you an image of your dream project — then actualise it, employing the latest technology to deliver a complete, ready-to-use facility.

  • Architectural & structural design
  • 3D visualisation & graphics for clarity
  • Single-point turnkey delivery
  • Projects of all sizes & complexity

General & Maintenance Construction

Dependable general construction together with refurbishment, renovation and maintenance services that keep buildings, roads and structures continuously serving your changing needs — backed by our own plant and machinery.

  • New build & civil works
  • Refurbishment & renovation
  • Drainage, fencing & site works
  • Ongoing facility maintenance
Sectors we serve

Expertise across five industries

We deliver excellent work and have leveraged on it to grow our client base through recommendations.

Residential

Private residences, apartments, town houses and estates — from single homes to multi-storey developments.

Commercial
